# Grey areas and points to verify

> **Unofficial English guide.** Condensed from the Vietnamese pages listed below; the Vietnamese version prevails. Vietnamese laws have no official English translation — renderings follow the [glossary](glossary.md). **Synced with Vietnamese version:** 28/09/2026.
> **Vietnamese source:** [docs/00-tong-quan/diem-can-doi-chieu.md](../docs/00-tong-quan/diem-can-doi-chieu.md)

This page lists the gray areas, gaps and conflicts between instruments found while building the toolkit (source texts checked on 24/09/2026). Each point is also marked **[TO VERIFY]** (*[CẦN ĐỐI CHIẾU]*) in the detailed page concerned. Use it as a list of questions for the competent authority or counsel, and as a watch list for the implementing circulars of the Ministry of Public Security (MPS). IDs match the Vietnamese page.

Instruments: Law 116 (Cybersecurity Law 2025), Decrees 330, 331 and 333/2026, PDPL (Law 91/2025), Decree 356/2025, Resolution 22/2026, TCVN 14423:2026.

### C18. Level 1–2 procedure (reviewed 28/09/2026)

**Provisions:** Art. 3.2, 4.3, 18.1, 18.4, 20.3(a), 23, 24, 30.6, 31.1(a), 31.2(a), 32.4, 33.1, 36.10 Decree 331; Appendix Forms 01, 06; Art. 43 Law on Enterprises 2020.

| Sub-issue | Analysis in the source | Toolkit's working position |
|---|---|---|
| (i) Approval instrument | Authority is clear: the designated cybersecurity unit (*đơn vị chuyên trách về an ninh mạng*) appraises, approves and then "sends a report" to the system owner (Art. 18.1, 20.3(a)); the power is conferred directly by the decree (Art. 32.4 "within its competence"), not delegated (Art. 4.3 is a different mechanism). Only the **form** is missing: Form 06 is designed for the system owner to sign (Levels 3–4); Form 01, sent to the designated unit requesting "appraisal and approval", is the Level 1–2 input form. A Decision fits Art. 36.10 (annual report cites the "approval Decision"). Seal: Art. 43 Law on Enterprises 2020 lets enterprises decide seals of "other units"; Decree 30/2020 applies to state bodies and SOEs. Precedent (from a legal Q&A source, full text not checked): Art. 12.1, 14.3(a) Decree 85/2016 had a similar mechanism. | The designated unit signs the Decision in its own name (not "on behalf of"/"by authorization"); the assignment decision states the signatory, document numbering and seal use. |
| (ii) Time limits | Art. 23.2 (05 working days to respond to an incomplete dossier) applies to all levels; Art. 23.3 sets no appraisal time limit for Levels 1–2; **Art. 24.2 (07 working days) also applies to Levels 1–2**, since Art. 24.1(b) has to say "Level 3 and above" separately. For Levels 1–2 the approval dossier = the proposal dossier (Art. 24.1(a)), received by the same unit, so "receipt of a complete valid dossier" may coincide with receipt for appraisal. | Appraisal + approval within a **total of 07 working days** from receipt of a complete valid dossier (appraisal ≤ 05 days — an internal target set by the toolkit, not a statutory limit). |
| (iii) Art. 18.4 | It replaces only the appraiser, not the approver; literally the designated unit still approves (a conflict of interest, not a gap in authority). | When Art. 18.4 applies: an independent unit/council appraises and the **head of the organization signs** the approval (Art. 31.1(a), 31.2(a)). |
| (iv) Art. 33.1 | No real conflict: at Levels 1–2 the approved object is the dossier including the security plan (Art. 18.1, 21.4, 23.1(b)–(c), 30.6), approved by a unit "of the system owner" (Art. 3.2); only Level 5 has a separate plan approval (Art. 18.3(d), Form 07). | The assignment decision states that the plan approved under Art. 18.1 is the plan "approved by the system owner" for Art. 33.1. |

## D. TCVN 14423:2026

- The standard is copyrighted. The toolkit only summarizes it with clause numbers, so **buy the official copy from VSQI** before signing off a security plan.
- Checklists are **not strict supersets** of lower levels. Examples: Level 4 (6.17.2.1) drops the point about a contact with the state management agency; Level 3 (5.12.2.5) does not require anti-malware on remote-access devices. Each level is therefore listed in full.
- Unresolved whether Appendix A (physical security) is "normative" or "informative". Art. 30.2 Decree 331 does not count physical security among the basic requirements.
