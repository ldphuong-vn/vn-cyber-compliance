This page is for foreign executives (general directors, regional CISOs, board members) who are asked to **sign or approve** a Vietnamese document. It does **not** translate the forms. It explains, part by part, what each section is, what the drafter fills in, and what you should check before you sign. Short glosses of headings are given in quotes (for example "Kính gửi" = "To") so you can find your way around the Vietnamese page. The document itself stays in Vietnamese, and the Vietnamese text is what binds the company.

Where to find the documents: the list of Forms 01–08 (who signs, where each goes) is in [02-security-levels.md §12](02-security-levels.md). Internal decisions and submissions are listed in [07-governance-inspection-reporting.md](07-governance-inspection-reporting.md) (§2, §6), and the simplified Level 1–2 set is in [08-level-1-2-toolkit.md](08-level-1-2-toolkit.md). All templates are catalogued in [09-templates-catalog.md](09-templates-catalog.md). This page does not repeat those tables.

## 1. Anatomy of a Vietnamese administrative document

The Forms in the Decree 331 Appendix and the toolkit's internal templates all use the standard layout of Vietnamese administrative documents. For state bodies and state-owned enterprises, the format is set by the rules on clerical work in Decree 30/2020/NĐ-CP. Other enterprises use it only as a reference. The Vietnamese pages note that Decree 30/2020 is not in the repository's source set [TO VERIFY]. Private companies may use their own internal format, as long as the document shows the signing authority, legal basis, content, effective date and recipients ([docs/04 README §5](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/README.md)). For seals, the Law on Enterprises 2020 Art. 43 lets an enterprise decide the type, number and form of seals of the enterprise and of its "other units", including digital signatures (see [08-level-1-2-toolkit.md](08-level-1-2-toolkit.md) and gray-area C18 in [gray-areas.md](gray-areas.md)).

A typical first page, top to bottom:

| # | Element (where it sits) | Vietnamese marker | What it is | What to check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Issuing body block** (top left) | Company name in capitals; for a department's document, the company name on the first line and the department below | The legal author of the document. Forms 06, 07 and 08 carry "(CHỦ QUẢN HỆ THỐNG THÔNG TIN)" here, which means the **system owner** (*chủ quản hệ thống thông tin*) issues them | The issuer is the body that holds the power. A Level 3–4 approval must come from the system owner, not from the IT department |
| 2 | **National motto** (top right) | "CỘNG HÒA XÃ HỘI CHỦ NGHĨA VIỆT NAM / Độc lập - Tự do - Hạnh phúc" = "Socialist Republic of Vietnam / Independence - Freedom - Happiness" | A fixed heading with no legal content of its own | Nothing to check |
| 3 | **Number and symbol** (under the issuer) | "Số: 40/2026/QĐ-TURBO" = number / year / document type-issuer abbreviation | The registration number from the company's document register. Type codes seen in the toolkit: **QĐ** = decision, **TTr** = submission, **BC** = report; a department's code may be added (e.g. "03/2026/QĐ-PANM", the Cybersecurity Department's register in the sample data) | The number is registered, not left blank ("Số: …/…") on the copy you sign. Other documents cite this number, so it must match everywhere |
| 4 | **Subject line** for letters (under the number) | "V/v …" = "Re: …" | Used in official letters (*công văn*). These have no type name in the centre; Forms 01–04 and 08 are letters of this kind | The subject matches what you are actually asked to do (appraisal only, or appraisal *and* approval) |
| 5 | **Place and date** (top right, italic) | "*Hà Nội, ngày … tháng … năm …*" = "Hanoi, day … month … year …" | Date of signature | Dates must follow the legal sequence, for example regulation issued → appraisal minutes → approval decision (Art. 30.7 Decree 331) |
| 6 | **Document type and summary** (centre, bold) | **QUYẾT ĐỊNH** (decision), **TỜ TRÌNH** (internal submission), **BÁO CÁO** (report), **BIÊN BẢN** (minutes), each followed by "Về việc …" = "On …" | Tells you what legal act you are performing. A decision creates obligations; a submission only asks for them; a report informs | You are signing the type you think you are signing |
| 7 | **Authority line** (decisions only, centre, under the title) | Title of the signer in capitals, e.g. "TỔNG GIÁM ĐỐC" (General Director) | Names the office that exercises the power | It matches your office and the signature block |
| 8 | **Addressee** (letters, submissions, reports) | "Kính gửi: …" = "To: …" | The body that must act on the document | The right body for the security level (see §2 below) |
| 9 | **Legal bases** (italic lines) | "Căn cứ …" = "Pursuant to …"; "Xét đề nghị của …" = "Considering the proposal of …" | The laws, the company charter or internal rules that give you the power, and the internal document that triggered this one | Every instrument is cited with its **full number and date** (e.g. Law No. 116/2025/QH15; Decree No. 331/2026/NĐ-CP of 19/8/2026). The cited internal decisions and minutes exist and carry the numbers shown |
| 10 | **Operative words** (decisions) | "QUYẾT ĐỊNH:" = "DECIDES:" | Start of the operative part | — |
| 11 | **Articles** | "Điều 1, Điều 2, …" = Article; clauses "1., 2."; points "a), b)" | Decisions usually run: Art. 1 what is approved or established; Art. 2 implementation and responsibilities; last Art. effective date and who executes it ("Quyết định này có hiệu lực kể từ ngày ký" = "effective from the date of signature") | Named units and people are correct. Deadlines and amounts are stated. The effective date is right, and any document being replaced is named |
| 12 | **Distribution list** (bottom left) | "Nơi nhận:" = "Recipients:"; "Như trên" = "as addressed above"; "Như Điều 3" = "those named in Art. 3"; "(để b/c)" = "for reporting"; "Lưu: VT, …" = "filed at the records office and …" | Who receives copies. This is also the evidence of notification | Everyone with a duty under the document is listed, including the operating unit and the designated cybersecurity unit |
| 13 | **Signature block** (bottom right) | Capacity line, office title, then "(Ký, ghi rõ họ tên, chức danh và đóng dấu)" = "(Sign, full name, title and seal)" | See the capacity prefixes below | The right person in the right capacity, and a seal only if your records rules require one |
| 14 | **Seal** | Red company seal over the signature, or a digital signature | Authenticates the document under the company's seal rules (Law on Enterprises 2020 Art. 43) | A seal does not replace signing authority. The Vietnamese pages warn against using a seal stamped on a document without the signature of the person who holds the power (*dấu treo*) as a substitute for that authority |

**Capacity prefixes in the signature block.** These are general conventions of Vietnamese administrative practice. The toolkit pages use them but do not define them, and their legal source (Decree 30/2020) is not in the source set [TO VERIFY]:

| Prefix | Short gloss | When you see it |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | Signs in own name | The head of the body signs personally |
| **TM.** | "on behalf of" a collective body | e.g. the chair signs for a board or council |
| **KT.** | "signing for" the head | A deputy signs in place of the head (e.g. a deputy general director for the general director) |
| **TL.** | "by order of" the head | A head of department or office signs by order of the head |
| **TUQ.** | "by authorization of" the head | A person signs under a specific written authorization |

The prefix matters for Level 1–2 approvals. The designated cybersecurity unit approves Level 1–2 dossiers under a power given directly by Decree 331 (Art. 18.1, 20.3(a), 32.4). It therefore signs **in its own name, not "TL./TUQ." of the general director**. Otherwise the approval becomes a document of the system owner, which is the wrong body under Art. 18.1 ([docs/08 file 07](../docs/08-bo-mau-cap-1-2/07-qd-phe-duyet-cap-do.md); C18). By contrast, the short incident-report template uses "TUQ." on purpose, because the decision assigning cybersecurity duties authorizes the incident contact to sign for the head of the organization.

## 2. Decree 331 Forms 01–08: section-by-section reading aids

The Forms are reproduced verbatim from the Decree 331 Appendix in the Vietnamese pages. Blanks are shown as `{{...}}`. The assignment of each form to a level or procedural step is **inferred** from its addressee and content; the Decree does not say which form is used at which step [TO VERIFY]. For the purpose, level, signer and recipient of each form, see [02-security-levels.md §12](02-security-levels.md); §12.1 there lists the mismatches between the Forms and the Decree body.

### Forms 01, 02, 03: requests (letters)

These three share one layout and differ mainly in addressee and purpose:

- **Form 01:** the operating unit (*đơn vị vận hành*) asks the designated cybersecurity unit (*đơn vị chuyên trách về an ninh mạng*) for appraisal **and** approval. Used for Level 1–2 (Art. 18.1, 20.1(b)).
- **Form 02:** a request for appraisal. For Level 3 it goes to the designated or independent unit (Art. 18.2(a), 18.4). For Levels 4–5 the system owner sends it to the MPS, or to the MND or Government Cipher Committee for their systems (Art. 18.3, 20.1(d)).
- **Form 03:** before the Level 4–5 dossier leaves the company, the operating unit asks the designated cybersecurity unit for a professional opinion (Art. 20.1(d), 21.5).

| Part / heading (VN, short) | What it contains | What the signer should check |
|---|---|---|
| Number, "V/v" subject line | Form 01: appraisal and approval; Form 02: appraisal; Form 03: professional opinion | The right form for the proposed level |
| "Kính gửi" (To) | Designated cybersecurity unit (01, 03); appraising body (02) | For Levels 1–3: if the designated unit also operates the system, the request goes to the independent unit or council (Art. 18.4) |
| "Căn cứ" (Pursuant to) | Law 116, then the implementing decrees and internal rules | Decree 331 is cited with number and date |
| "Phần 1. Thông tin chung" (General information) | System name, operating unit, address, **proposed level** | The level matches the level proposal statement |
| "Phần 2. Hồ sơ kèm theo" (Attached dossier) | 4 items: general description, design documents, level proposal statement, security plan statement; Form 02 adds item 5, the professional opinion (Levels 4–5 only) | All items are attached. For a new project, item 2 should name the preliminary design actually used (Art. 21.2(a)) [TO VERIFY]. For Level 3, Form 02 item 5 is marked "not applicable" |
| Footnote ¹ (Form 02 only) | Optional request to include the system in the List of information systems critical to national security | Add it only if the system has grounds under Art. 16, 17 |
| Closing sentence | Restates the request | Form 02's lead-in says "appraisal and approval" but its closing asks only for an appraisal opinion; this comes from the Decree's form and is kept as written |
| "Nơi nhận" + signature block | Representative of the requesting organization, with a seal | Form 01, 03: usually the head of the operating unit. Form 02 for Levels 4–5: the system owner's representative |

Keep the proof of sending and receipt. The 05-working-day period for a reply on an incomplete dossier runs from the date the dossier is received (Art. 23.2).

### Form 04: appraisal opinion

**Who to whom:** the appraising body (the designated unit or independent unit/council for Levels 1–3; the MPS, MND or Government Cipher Committee for Levels 4–5) sends it to the system owner or the operating unit. **When:** within 15 working days (Level 3) or 25 working days (Levels 4–5) of receiving a complete, valid dossier (Art. 23.3). It is a **mandatory part of the approval dossier for Level 3 and above** (Art. 24.1(b)).

| Part / heading (VN, short) | What it contains | What the signer should check |
|---|---|---|
| Opening paragraph | "Received letter No. … dated …" of the requester | The number and date match the Form 02 request that was sent |
| "Phần 1" (Documents appraised) | The 5 dossier items | The version appraised is the version you will approve |
| "Phần 2" (Legal basis for appraisal) | Law 116, implementing texts, other | — |
| "Phần 3" (Organization of appraisal) | Lead unit, coordinating units, method (meeting and/or written opinions) | For Level 1–3, the lead unit is independent of the operating unit |
| "Phần 4" (Appraisal opinion) | 4.1 summary of coordinating units' opinions (Levels 4–5); 4.2 opinion on the suitability of the proposed level; 4.3 other | The form mentions only the level. Art. 23.1 also requires an opinion on the security plan in design and in operation, so check that all three points (a)–(c) are covered |
| "Phần 5. Kết luận" (Conclusion) | "phù hợp" (suitable) or "chưa phù hợp" (not yet suitable). If not suitable, the unsuitable points must be listed | Do not approve on a "chưa phù hợp" opinion. Send the dossier back for adjustment |

**For Levels 1–2**, the toolkit replaces Form 04 with internal appraisal minutes (*biên bản thẩm định*). The Vietnamese pages treat Form 04 as mandatory only from Level 3 (Art. 24.1(b)), but the appraisal must still be evidenced ([docs/08 file 06](../docs/08-bo-mau-cap-1-2/06-bien-ban-tham-dinh.md)). The minutes have four parts: I. participants; II. dossier; III. appraisal content under Art. 23.1; IV. overall conclusion. Check that the person who prepared the dossier did not sign as appraiser.

### Form 05: internal submission for approval

**Who to whom:** the operating unit sends it to the system owner. The header reads "Kính gửi: (Cơ quan liên quan có thẩm quyền)" (the competent body), which is the system owner in practice. **When:** after a "suitable" appraisal opinion, for Levels 3–4 (Art. 20.3(b)). For Level 5, and for Level 3–4 systems in the national-security List (Art. 20.4), it may be used to submit the security plan with a draft Form 07 [TO VERIFY].

| Part / heading (VN, short) | What it contains | What the signer should check |
|---|---|---|
| Title "TỜ TRÌNH Về việc phê duyệt đề xuất cấp độ" | Submission for approval of the proposed level | — |
| "Căn cứ" | Law 116, decrees, **the appraisal opinion** | The appraisal opinion is cited by number and date. The toolkit also recommends citing the number and date of the decision issuing the cybersecurity regulation (Art. 30.7) |
| "Phần 1" (General information) | System, operating unit, address, proposed level | The level is the one the appraisal found suitable |
| "Phần 2" (Attached dossier) | Items 1–4; item 5, the professional opinion (Levels 4–5); item 6, the appraisal opinion (Level 3 and above) | Item 6 says "coordinating" appraising body, but Art. 24.1(b) requires the opinion of the **lead** appraising body. Attach the lead body's Form 04 |
| Closing + signature | Asks the system owner to decide | A draft Form 06 is attached for signature. The 07-working-day processing period runs from receipt of a complete, valid dossier (Art. 24.2) |

### Form 06: decision approving the security level (signed by the system owner)

This is the document most often placed in front of a foreign general director. It **approves the security level and the cybersecurity assurance plan** (*phương án bảo đảm an ninh mạng*) of one system. It is issued by the head of the system owner for Level 3 (Art. 18.2(b)) and Level 4 (Art. 18.3(d)). For an enterprise, the system owner is the management level that decides the investment. The signer is usually the legal representative, or a person authorized in writing under Art. 4.3.

| Part / heading (VN, short) | What it contains | What the signer should check |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer block | Name of the system owner | Matches the decision identifying the system owner |
| "QUYẾT ĐỊNH Về việc phê duyệt cấp độ hệ thống thông tin" + authority line | Decision approving the security level; title of the head (e.g. "TỔNG GIÁM ĐỐC") | Your title as it appears in the charter or authorization |
| "Căn cứ" / "Xét đề nghị của" | Law 116, implementing decrees; the requesting unit's proposal | Decree 331 is cited with its number. The template's words "Nghị định này" ("this Decree") should be replaced by the number when issued |
| **Art. 1.1** General information | System name, operating unit, address | Same as in Forms 02/04/05 |
| **Art. 1.2** Security level | "Cấp độ …" (Level …) | Same as the appraisal conclusion. This is the core of your approval |
| **Art. 1.3** Security plan | (a) the plan **in design** and (b) the plan **in operation** conform to the national standard and national technical regulation for the level | Standard = TCVN 14423:2026. The toolkit suggests also naming the clause for the level (Level 3: clause 5; Level 4: clause 6). If no national technical regulation exists, the toolkit writes "(không có)" (none) [TO VERIFY] |
| **Art. 2** Implementation | The operating unit's duties under Art. 33 Decree 331; other duties; duties of other bodies | Named units are correct, and any conditions or remediation deadlines you require are written here |
| **Art. 3** Enforcement | The requesting unit executes; the designated cybersecurity unit monitors and reports to the system owner | The monitoring duty is assigned to a real unit |
| "Nơi nhận" + signature + seal | Representative of the organization | Signed personally or under a valid written authorization |

By signing, the system owner takes on a legal commitment. Level 3–5 systems may not go live before the level is approved, and the approved plan must be fully implemented before operation (Art. 30.6). Operating before approval carries an organization fine of VND 40–60 million (Art. 23.1(c) Decree 330). After signing, the decision number feeds Form 08 columns (6)–(7).

### Form 07: decision approving the cybersecurity assurance plan

It has the same layout as Form 06, but Art. 1 approves **only the security plan** (in design and in operation), not the level. **Used for** Level 5 systems, whose level and List entry are approved by the Prime Minister (Art. 18.3(đ)), and for Level 3–4 systems in the national-security List (Art. 20.4). **Signed by** the head of the system owner (Art. 18.3(d), 20.3(c)). **Check:**

- The MPS, MND or Government Cipher Committee appraisal opinion (Form 04) is attached.
- The Prime Minister's List decision is cited by number and date.
- The order between the two steps is not specified in the Decree [TO VERIFY].
- Systems in the List are also subject to cybersecurity appraisal (Art. 5 Decree 333) and to assessment and certification before operation (Art. 9.3 Law 116; Art. 6 Decree 333).

### Form 08: annual report to the MPS

**Who to whom:** the system owner reports on all its information systems. The form is addressed to the designated cybersecurity unit or the specialized cybersecurity protection force; under Art. 35.4(b) the owner sends the report to the **Ministry of Public Security (MPS)**. The original form has **no signature block**; the toolkit recommends adding "Nơi nhận" and a signature block in the usual format [TO VERIFY].

**Timeline (Art. 35 Decree 331):**

| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Data period | **15/12** of the previous year to **14/12** of the reporting year (Art. 35.3) |
| Designated cybersecurity unit and operating unit → system owner | before **20/12** each year (Art. 35.4(a)) |
| System owner → MPS | before **25/12** each year (Art. 35.4(b)) |
| Frequency | Annual, plus ad hoc (Art. 35.2) |
| Channels | Document management system, MPS reporting software, email, other (Art. 35.1) |

**Sections 1–2:**

| Part / heading (VN, short) | What it contains | What the signer should check |
|---|---|---|
| "1. Thông tin chung" (General information) | Counts: total systems in scope; classified; level dossiers prepared; appraised; approved; approved and periodically inspected; **expected date for approving all systems** | The counts reconcile with the Section 3 table. The expected completion date is one you are prepared to be held to |
| "2. a)" System owner | Name; document setting its functions (leave blank if none); head and title; address; phone and email | Matches the decision identifying the system owner |
| "2. b)" Designated cybersecurity unit | Name; document setting its functions; head and title; address; contacts | The appointment decision exists and is cited |

**Section 3 table: one row per system, 12 columns:**

| Col. | Heading (VN, short) | Content / allowed values |
|---|---|---|
| (1) | STT | Row number |
| (2)–(4) | Tên HTTT / Chủ quản / Đơn vị vận hành | System name; system owner; operating unit |
| (5) | Cấp độ đề xuất | Proposed level, 1–5 |
| (6) | Tình trạng phê duyệt | Approval status, one of four fixed values: "Đang dự thảo" (drafting), "Đã gửi thẩm định" (sent for appraisal), "Đã thẩm định" (appraised), "Đã được phê duyệt" (approved) |
| (7) | Quyết định phê duyệt cấp độ | Number, date and approving body of the level approval decision |
| (8) | Quy chế bảo đảm ANM | Number, date and issuing body of the decision issuing the cybersecurity regulation (internal) |
| (9) | Dự kiến thời điểm phê duyệt | Expected approval date (systems not yet approved) |
| (10) | Đã triển khai đầy đủ PA | Security plan implementation: "Chưa triển khai" (not started), "Đã triển khai một số hạng mục" (partly), "Đã triển khai đầy đủ" (fully) |
| (11) | Dự kiến thời điểm triển khai đầy đủ | Expected date of full implementation |
| (12) | Đã kiểm tra, đánh giá ANM | Inspection/assessment done: "Trước khi đưa vào sử dụng" (before go-live), "Định kỳ" (periodic), both, or blank |

**What the signer should check:** the fixed values in columns (6), (10) and (12) are used exactly as listed, so the MPS can aggregate them. Every "approved" row has a decision number in (7), and every approved row has a regulation in (8). Form 08 does not cover everything Art. 36 requires, for example systems with grounds for the national-security List (Art. 36.3), compliance with the regulation in operation (Art. 36.7), and implementation assessed per criterion (Art. 36.9, 36.10). The toolkit recommends attaching an annex covering Art. 36. Details and preparation calendar: [07-governance-inspection-reporting.md §5](07-governance-inspection-reporting.md).

## 3. Internal decisions, the Level 1–2 approval decision and internal submissions

### 3.1 Internal decisions (docs/04)

These decisions set up the governance on which every form depends. Their list, signers and order are in [07-governance-inspection-reporting.md §2](07-governance-inspection-reporting.md). They share the layout in §1: issuer, "QUYẾT ĐỊNH Về việc …", "Căn cứ" (Law 116, Decree 331, and the company charter or internal rules as the source of the signer's power), "Xét …" (the submission that proposed it), Articles, and an effective date "từ ngày ký" (from signature).

| Decision | Operative articles (what to read) | What the approver should verify |
|---|---|---|
| Identifying the system owner / delegating owner duties ([VN](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/qd-chi-dinh-chu-quan-uy-quyen.md)) | Art. 1 names the system owner for each system (annex list); Art. 2 (optional) delegates duties under Art. 31.2 to a subordinate organization with sufficient capacity (Art. 4.3); Art. 3 implementation; Art. 4 effect, and any document it replaces | Every system in scope is in the annex. The delegate is a subordinate organization, and the scope and term are stated. All later Forms will quote this name |
| Establishing/appointing the designated cybersecurity unit ([VN](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/qd-chi-dinh-don-vi-bo-phan-chuyen-trach-anm.md)) | Art. 1 establishes or appoints the designated cybersecurity unit or team; Art. 2 duties and powers (Art. 32 Decree 331); Art. 3 organization and staff; Art. 4 effect | The unit is independent of operations, or the Art. 18.4 route is planned. Staffing is real. Failing to assign staff carries an organization fine of VND 60–100 million (Art. 23.2(c) Decree 330) |
| Assigning the operating unit ([VN](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/qd-giao-don-vi-van-hanh.md)) | Art. 1 assigns the operating unit (annex by component if outsourced); Art. 2 duties (Art. 33 Decree 331); Art. 3 effect (for outsourcing: until the contract ends) | The contract number is cited when a vendor operates the system |
| Establishing an independent appraisal council ([VN](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/qd-thanh-lap-hoi-dong-tham-dinh.md)) | Art. 1 establishes the council under Art. 18.4(b) with a member table (chair, vice-chair, reviewers, members, non-voting secretary); following Articles set tasks and working rules | No member prepared, administers or supplies the system. The Vietnamese page recommends at least one member with in-depth cybersecurity competence (Decree 331 sets no council composition [TO VERIFY]). Council members should not also carry out the self-assessment (Art. 31.2(c)) |
| Issuing the cybersecurity regulation (*quy chế bảo đảm an ninh mạng*, ≈ information security policy) ([VN](../docs/04-chinh-sach-quy-trinh/quy-che-bao-dam-anm.md)) | Part A is a short issuing decision: Art. 1 issues the regulation attached; Art. 2 effective date and replaced documents; Art. 3 who executes. Part B is the regulation itself, in chapters following Art. 30.3 Decree 331 | The issuing date is **before** the level approval date (Art. 30.7). Parameters match the highest level covered. Not issuing it carries an organization fine of VND 40–60 million (Art. 23.1(a) Decree 330) |

### 3.2 Level 1–2 approval decision (docs/08, file 07)

Decree 331 has no approval form for Levels 1–2. The toolkit keeps the **structure of Form 06** but makes the designated cybersecurity unit the issuer ([VN](../docs/08-bo-mau-cap-1-2/07-qd-phe-duyet-cap-do.md) · [docx](../templates/08-bo-mau-cap-1-2/07-qd-phe-duyet-cap-do.docx)). The file contains **two documents signed by the same person**: the decision, and a report to the system owner (Art. 18.1 requires the unit to "send a report" to the owner after approving).

| Part | What it contains | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer block | Company name, then the designated unit (e.g. Cybersecurity Department) | The unit named in the decision assigning cybersecurity duties ([VN](../docs/08-bo-mau-cap-1-2/02-qd-phan-cong-anm.md)); its Art. 1.3 names who signs and how the seal is used |
| "Căn cứ" | Law 116; Decree 331 (Art. 18.1, 20.3(a), 32.4); decision assigning cybersecurity duties; regulation-issuing decision; appraisal minutes; operating unit's request letter (Form 01) | All five internal documents exist and their numbers and dates are correct |
| Art. 1 | Operating unit, dossier version; **table** of systems (code, name, level, applicable TCVN 14423:2026 clause: Level 1 → clause 3, Level 2 → clause 4); security plan in design and in operation | No system shows signs of Level 3 or above (Art. 13, 8.2). If one does, it is removed and the Level 3 procedure is followed. The standard's name is written without a clause number |
| Art. 2 | Operating unit duties (Art. 33); keep the plan implemented and ask for re-determination when Art. 10.2 applies; **remediation table** (gap, system, deadline) | Each gap has an owner and a deadline and is carried into the annual plan. Only gaps that do not change the level belong here |
| Art. 3 | Effective from signature; the designated unit monitors and reports | — |
| Signature | Head of the designated unit, **in its own name** (no TL./TUQ.) | If Art. 18.4 applied (independent appraisal because the unit also operates), the **head of the system owner** signs instead, and the decision then resembles Form 06 |
| Page 2: "BÁO CÁO" to the system owner | Process (request, appraisal minutes, decision; the regulation was issued first), results table, remediation roadmap, recommendations | Signed on the same day as the decision or right after. Its figures are reused in Form 08 |

Timing: the toolkit reads the 07-working-day limit of Art. 24.2 as covering Levels 1–2, with appraisal and approval together within 07 working days of a complete, valid dossier (C18). See [08-level-1-2-toolkit.md](08-level-1-2-toolkit.md).

### 3.3 Internal submissions (*tờ trình*, docs/07)

A submission is an internal memo from a department asking management to approve something. It is not filed with the State. The nine templates are listed in [07-governance-inspection-reporting.md §6](07-governance-inspection-reporting.md). All share one structure ([docs/07 README §3](../docs/07-to-trinh-lanh-dao/README.md)):

| Part (VN heading) | What it contains | What an approver should verify |
|---|---|---|
| Header: number "…/TTr-[dept]", title "TỜ TRÌNH Về việc …", "Kính gửi" (To), "Căn cứ" | The requesting department and the decision-maker (e.g. the general director); laws, charter or internal rules, and supporting findings (e.g. a self-assessment result) | You are the right decision-maker under the charter. The cited findings exist |
| **I. SỰ CẦN THIẾT** (Need) | 1. legal basis with article citations; current situation; risks if nothing is done | Each obligation cites an exact article. Fines are stated as **organization** amounts. In the cybersecurity articles of Decree 330 the printed amount is for individuals and organizations pay double (Art. 7.1 Decree 330); fines are not added up into one "total risk" figure. Deadlines are applied to the right systems, e.g. the 30/06/2027 transition date only for the systems it covers (Art. 45.1 Law 116; Art. 39.1 Decree 331) |
| **II. NỘI DUNG ĐỀ XUẤT** (Proposal) | What is proposed: organization, documents, solutions, contracts | The scope matches the need. Nothing is claimed that goes beyond the law; for example, an online service is not automatically Level 3 |
| **III. KINH PHÍ DỰ KIẾN** (Estimated budget) | Cost table: one-off and annual cost, source of funds | In the templates all figures are placeholders. Check quotes, VAT, recurring costs and the budget line |
| **IV. TIẾN ĐỘ** (Timeline) | Milestones table | Milestones meet legal deadlines, e.g. the regulation before level approval (Art. 30.7) and controls in place before go-live (Art. 30.6) |
| **V. TỔ CHỨC THỰC HIỆN** (Implementation) | Assignments to departments (IT, cybersecurity, legal, HR, finance) | Each task has a responsible unit, and independence rules are respected (Art. 18.4, 31.2(c)) |
| **VI. KIẾN NGHỊ** (Recommendation) | "Kính trình … xem xét, phê duyệt: 1… 2…" = "Respectfully submitted for approval: 1… 2…" | This numbered list is **what you actually approve**. Read it item by item |
| "Nơi nhận", signature of the submitting head | — | — |
| **"Ý KIẾN PHÊ DUYỆT CỦA LÃNH ĐẠO"** (Management's decision) | Tick boxes: approve all / approve phase 1 only / reject / other; comments; date and signature | Tick one box, write any conditions in the comment line, then date and sign. An approved submission is usually followed by a **decision** that you also sign |

## 4. Before you sign: checklist for executives

Grounded in the Vietnamese guidance pages. Fines are organization amounts under Decree 330.

1. **Right document type and right signer.** Level 3–4 level approval (Form 06) and Level 5 plan approval (Form 07) are signed by the head of the system owner, or a person authorized in writing under Art. 4.3 Decree 331. Level 1–2 approval is signed by the designated cybersecurity unit in its own name (Art. 18.1, 20.3(a)), unless Art. 18.4 applies.
2. **Cybersecurity regulation already issued.** The regulation must be approved and issued **before** the level dossier is approved (Art. 30.7 Decree 331). Check its decision number and date, which must be earlier than today's decision. Otherwise the fine is VND 40–60 million (Art. 23.1(a) Decree 330).
3. **Appraisal opinion attached (Level ≥ 3).** The approval dossier must include the appraisal opinion of the **lead** appraising body (Art. 24.1(b)), and its conclusion must be "phù hợp" (suitable). For Levels 1–2, signed appraisal minutes take its place.
4. **Appraisal was independent.** If the designated cybersecurity unit also operates the system, a subordinate unit or an independent appraisal council must have appraised it (Art. 18.4). The person who prepared the dossier must not have appraised it.
5. **Professional opinion attached (Levels 4–5).** The designated unit's opinion on the level and the plan is part of the dossier (Art. 20.1(d), 21.5).
6. **Same level everywhere.** The request (Form 01/02), the level proposal statement, the appraisal opinion and the decision all state the same level. A Level 1–2 decision must not include any system showing Level 3 signals (Art. 13, 8.2).
7. **Standard reference matches the level.** TCVN 14423:2026 clause 3/4/5/6/7 for Levels 1/2/3/4/5, and the national technical regulation named, or marked as none [TO VERIFY].
8. **Within the time limit.** Approval is processed within **07 working days** of a complete, valid dossier (Art. 24.2); for Levels 1–2 the toolkit counts appraisal inside those 07 days (C18). Dates run in order: regulation → appraisal → decision → report to owner.
9. **Legal bases complete and exact.** Law No. 116/2025/QH15 and Decree No. 331/2026/NĐ-CP of 19/8/2026 are cited by number (not "this Decree"). Internal decisions, minutes and letters are cited with correct numbers and dates.
10. **Go-live conditions understood.** Do not let a Level 3–5 system go live before approval, and implement the approved plan fully before operation (Art. 30.6). Otherwise the fine is VND 40–60 million (Art. 23.1(c)–(d) Decree 330). Failing to organize appraisal and approval also carries VND 40–60 million (Art. 24.1 Decree 330).
11. **Budget and resources realistic.** For submissions, the recommendation list (Part VI) matches the budget table and the timeline, and recurring costs and the funding source are stated.
12. **Distribution, seal and filing.** "Nơi nhận" lists everyone with a duty. The seal or digital signature follows the company's records rules (Law on Enterprises 2020 Art. 43). The signed original goes into the system's evidence file, and the decision details feed Form 08 columns (6)–(8).
