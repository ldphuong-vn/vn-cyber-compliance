# Penalties under Decree 330

> **Unofficial English guide.** Condensed from the Vietnamese pages listed below; the Vietnamese version prevails. Vietnamese laws have no official English translation — renderings follow the [glossary](glossary.md). **Synced with Vietnamese version:** 28/09/2026.
> **Vietnamese source:** [docs/05-nghia-vu-lien-quan/nd-330-muc-phat.md](../docs/05-nghia-vu-lien-quan/nd-330-muc-phat.md)

Decree 330/2026 on administrative penalties for cybersecurity and personal data protection violations (*Decree 330 (Penalties Decree)*) covers cybersecurity (Chapter II, Sections 1–5) and personal data protection (Section 6), effective **19/8/2026** (Art. 80 Decree 330). This page selects violations directly relevant to organizational compliance: security levels, incidents, system logs, data localization, account verification and personal data. All figures were checked against the full text in the Vietnamese page; for a specific case, re-read the original article.

## 1. General principles

### 1.1. Reading the fine amounts — the most common mistake

| Field | Amount written in the article applies to | Conversion | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cybersecurity** (Sections 1–5, Art. 9–38) | **Individuals** | **Organization = 2×** | Art. 7.1 para. 1 Decree 330 |
| **Personal data protection** (Section 6, Art. 39–71) | **Organizations** | **Individual = 1/2** | Art. 7.1 para. 2 Decree 330 |

The tables below are **already converted to organization amounts**; for cybersecurity rows the individual amount written in the text is shown in brackets for look-up.

### 1.2. Maximum fines

| Field | Organization | Individual | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cybersecurity | VND 200,000,000 | VND 100,000,000 | Art. 7.3 Decree 330 |
| Personal data — buying/selling personal data | 10 times the revenue obtained; if no revenue or lower, the maximum of VND 03 billion applies | 1/2 of the organization amount | Art. 7.4(a), 7.4(d) Decree 330; Art. 8.3, 8.6 PDPL |
| Personal data — cross-border transfer violations | 5% of the preceding year's revenue; if no revenue or lower, the maximum of VND 03 billion applies | 1/2 | Art. 7.4(b) Decree 330; Art. 8.4 PDPL |
| Personal data — other violations | VND 03 billion | VND 1.5 billion | Art. 7.4(c)–(d) Decree 330; Art. 8.5–8.6 PDPL |

### 1.3. Other principles

| Topic | Rule | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who can be fined | Includes **both the system owner and the operating unit**; foreign enterprises and their branches/representative offices; household businesses are fined as individuals | Art. 2.2(k), 2.2(đ), 2.3 Decree 330 |
| Limitation period | **01 year**. End of a violation = the date the obligation is fulfilled, or the date the conduct actually ended as confirmed by the competent authority | Art. 3.1–3.2 |
| Main penalties | Warning; fine | Art. 4.1 |
| Additional penalties | Withdrawal of licenses (social network, general information website, cybersecurity product/service business, personal data processing service certificate…) or **suspension of operation for 01–24 months**; confiscation of exhibits and means; expulsion of foreigners | Art. 4.2 |
| Remedial measures | 10 groups: restoring the information system; recovering/returning data and resources; remedying loss of cybersecurity and leaks; removing violating elements in design, operation, personal data processing, contracts; public correction and apology; forced technical and management measures; honoring data subject rights; surrendering illegal gains… | Art. 5.1–5.10 |
| Gains from personal data violations | Buying/selling: total transaction value, **no deduction of costs**; unlawful collection/processing: **all actual revenue** from business activities using the data | Art. 6.2 |
| Overlap with other decrees | Violations in cybersecurity and personal data → Decree 330 applies | Art. 1.3 |
| Electronic sanctioning | Electronic evidence collected directly/online on the organization's information system is legally valid; minutes and decisions are digitally signed | Art. 8 |
| Transition | Conduct before 19/8/2026: the decree in force at the time applies, unless Decree 330 is more favorable | Art. 81.1 |
| Competence (examples) | Director of provincial Police: up to VND 200 million (cybersecurity), up to the maximum (personal data); Head of commune-level Police: up to VND 100 million (cybersecurity), 50% of the maximum (personal data) | Art. 72.2, 72.5 |

| # | Issue | Detail | Note |

| 1 | Two articles fine the absence of a security level proposal dossier | Art. 23.1(b) (Level 3–5, "not preparing a dossier") and Art. 24.1 ("not preparing a dossier … or not organizing appraisal and approval", any level) — same range VND 20–30 million (individual) | Same money in practice; different additional penalties on repeat (license withdrawal 01–06 months vs 01–03 months) |

## 5. How to use this table

- [ ] Compare each row with the organization's actual status; rows "not met" go into the remediation plan (see [07-governance-inspection-reporting.md](07-governance-inspection-reporting.md)).
- [ ] Prioritize rows carrying **forced stop of processing / suspension of service / suspension of operation** (Art. 29.3, 33.3, 55.3(b), 56.5–56.6 Decree 330) — their business impact exceeds the fine.
- [ ] Keep compliance evidence sufficient to prove compliance within the 01-year limitation period (Art. 3 Decree 330).
