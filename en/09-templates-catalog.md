This page lists **every file** in [templates/](../templates/README.md): 56 Word (.docx) and 5 Excel (.xlsx) files. For each one it gives an English title, its purpose, who prepares, signs and receives it, whether it is filed with an authority, and its main legal basis. The documents themselves are **not translated**. Use the Vietnamese files. To read a Vietnamese form before signing it, see [10-form-reading-guide.md](10-form-reading-guide.md).

## 1. How the files work

- **Generated from the Vietnamese pages.** Each Word file is built from the Vietnamese Markdown page with the same name in [docs/](../docs/), so the template and its guidance always match. The "how to fill" guidance stays in the Markdown page and does not appear in the Word file.
- **Layout.** Word files follow the Vietnamese administrative document format of Decree 30/2020 (*thể thức văn bản hành chính*, NĐ 30/2020/NĐ-CP): A4, Times New Roman 13, national header, number and reference, legal basis, recipients ("Nơi nhận") and signature block. Tables are resized to fit the page, and tables with 9 or more columns switch to landscape pages. Excel files have drop-down lists and summary formulas. Yellow cells are the ones to fill in.
- **Sample data.** Blanks are pre-filled with a **simulated** dossier of Công ty cổ phần Giải pháp Công nghệ TURBO and **highlighted yellow**. Only the company name is real. Personal names, departments, addresses, document numbers, IP ranges and figures are invented. Replace everything highlighted before use, and never commit real data to the public repository.
- **Placeholders.** In the Markdown sources, every blank is a `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` (e.g. `{{TEN_HE_THONG}}` = system name). To produce clean templates or your own data, edit `du-lieu-mau.json` (or set `CFG.sampleData = false` to keep the raw `{{...}}`) and regenerate:
  ```bash
  cd tools/md2docx && npm install && node build.js   # Word
  python3 tools/md2xlsx/build.py                      # Excel (run from the repository root)
  ```
  Details: [tools/md2docx/README.md](../tools/md2docx/README.md).
- **Why Vietnamese only.** Forms 01–08 are copied word for word from the Decree 331 Appendix. The other documents are decisions, regulations and memos signed inside a Vietnamese entity. An English version would have no legal standing and could drift from the official wording. **In practice, documents filed with Vietnamese authorities are prepared in Vietnamese** ([templates/README.md](../templates/README.md)); Forms 01–08 themselves are prescribed in Vietnamese in the Decree 331 Appendix. Files that cover TCVN 14423:2026 give only paraphrases and clause numbers. Check them against the official standard (sold by VSQI).
- **Suggested order** (templates/README): internal submissions (folder 07) → internal decisions and the **cybersecurity regulation** (folder 04; must be issued before level approval, Art. 30.7 Decree 331) → level determination (01) → dossier (02) → requirements (03) → annual Form 08. Organizations with only Level 1–2 systems use folder 08.

Column key: **Filed?** = *Yes — MPS* (sent to the Ministry of Public Security; for military systems the Ministry of National Defence, for Cipher Committee systems the Government Cipher Committee — Art. 18.3(b)–(c) Decree 331), *No — internal* (kept inside the organization), *Yes, on request* (kept internally and produced when an authority inspects or asks). Only the risk assessment record has an express production duty (Art. 10.6); for other documents "Yes, on request" is the toolkit's reading, based on the inspection scope of Art. 27 and the Form 08 reporting items (Art. 36). "Designated unit" = designated cybersecurity unit (*đơn vị chuyên trách về an ninh mạng*). "Operating unit" = operating unit of the information system (*đơn vị vận hành*). "Owner" = system owner (*chủ quản hệ thống thông tin*). All citations are to Decree 331 unless stated otherwise.

### 3.5 Folder 07 — Internal submissions to management (9 files)

Internal submissions (*tờ trình*: memos asking management to approve something) are **internal company documents, not filings with the State**. Each gives the legal basis, the current situation, the risk (organization fines), the proposal, budget, timeline and assignments, and ends with a box for management's decision. All are addressed to the company's management (`{{CHUC_DANH_LANH_DAO}}`, e.g. the General Director), who approves in the decision box.
